Messengers of Peace
Awards 2026

Recognising young Scouts who lead impactful community service projects and embody the values of Scouting.

The Messengers of Peace Awards celebrate leadership and global citizenship in four categories: peace, environment, health and well-being, and skills for life. We invite individuals, Scouts, and National Scout Organizations (NSOs) to nominate inspiring young Scouts aged 16–26.

Nominees must be registered Scouts and members of their NSO, leading a project that is ongoing or completed within the past two years (from May 2024 onwards). Self-nominations are not accepted.
